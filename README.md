# 🌟 Overview

BusinessLens AI is a business intelligence and planning application built around one central idea:

> **A business decision should be supported by evidence, assumptions, and calculations—not guesses.**

The platform combines:

- Market research
- Location research
- Competitor research
- Spreadsheet data
- Product analysis
- Pricing analysis
- Cost modelling
- Revenue modelling
- Profit calculations
- Break-even analysis
- Scenario modelling
- AI-assisted business advice
- Data confidence indicators
- Business reporting
- Presentation-ready dashboards

Instead of forcing users to use multiple spreadsheets, calculators, websites, and documents, BusinessLens AI brings the workflow together into a single interface.

# 🎯 Why BusinessLens AI Exists

Starting a business involves hundreds of decisions.

For example:

- Where should the business operate?
- Who are the customers?
- How much should products cost?
- Who are the competitors?
- What products are already available?
- What products might be missing?
- How much will rent cost?
- How much will employees cost?
- How much will electricity and water cost?
- How many customers are needed?
- What happens if sales are lower than expected?
- What happens if costs increase?
- When will the business break even?
- Which assumptions need more research?

BusinessLens AI organizes these questions into a structured decision-making workflow.

# 🧠 Core Concept

BusinessLens AI separates information into different categories.

## User Data

Information entered by the user.

Examples:

- Business type
- Location
- Budget
- Customers per day
- Average customer spending
- Rent
- Salaries
- Ingredient costs
- Product costs

---

## Demo Data

Sample information used to demonstrate the application.

Demo data should **never be mistaken for verified real-world information**.

---

## Calculated Data

Values generated mathematically from user inputs.

Examples:

- Monthly revenue
- Monthly expenses
- Monthly profit
- Break-even point
- Unit cost
- Suggested pricing

---

## AI Estimates

Values or recommendations generated by an AI system.

These should be treated as estimates rather than guaranteed facts.

---

## Needs Verification

Information that requires external research.

Examples:

- Current rent
- Local population
- Foot traffic
- Competitor prices
- Utility rates
- Labour costs
- Local regulations

# 📍 Location Analysis

Location can dramatically affect a business.

BusinessLens AI allows users to analyse information such as:

- Population
- Population density
- Average income
- Tourism
- Foot traffic
- Nearby businesses
- Nearby competitors
- Local customer demographics
- Rent
- Accessibility

The application is designed to distinguish between:

### Verified data

Information backed by a source.

### User-provided information

Information supplied directly by the user.

### Estimates

Values that have been estimated.

### Information requiring verification

Values that should be researched before making a real business decision.

# 🏪 Competitor Analysis

Competitor analysis allows users to understand the businesses already operating in a market.

A competitor can include:

- Direct competitors
- Indirect competitors
- Nearby businesses
- Businesses targeting the same customers
- Businesses selling similar products

The competitor table can include fields such as:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Business | Example Bakery |
| Distance | 0.8 km |
| Category | Bakery |
| Average Price | ฿120 |
| Rating | 4.5 |
| Products | Bread, cakes, coffee |

---

# 📊 Competitive Insights

Competitor data can help identify:

- Average prices
- Median prices
- Product categories
- Market gaps
- Competitive density
- Potential differentiation opportunities

The system should not simply copy competitors.

Instead, it can identify potential opportunities such as:

> "Several nearby competitors sell premium cakes, but fewer appear to focus on affordable breakfast products."

This can lead to a product strategy that is based on market gaps.
